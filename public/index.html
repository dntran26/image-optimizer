How it works

  1. Drop files inJPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC from an iPhone, or PDF. Drop as many as you like.
  2. OptimizeHit Optimize on a card, or Optimize All. Each card shows the exact size it will be first.
  3. DownloadOne by one, or all as a ZIP. Copies also land in images/optimized/.
